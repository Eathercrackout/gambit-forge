ℹ️ About & credits

Gambit Forge, an original army-building chess variant. Code, piece art and icon are original to this project.

Coach voices are pre-rendered offline with Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (model and voice packs licensed Apache-2.0), run through the MIT-licensed kokoro-onnx runtime. Per its model card, Kokoro was trained only on permissively licensed, public-domain and synthetic audio. No real person's voice was cloned. The TTS software is not shipped with the game; only the audio it produced is included.

Rules & Piece Glossary

Each side builds an army of 8 back-row and 8 pawn-row pieces with a 40-point budget; exactly one King (free, back row) is mandatory. White moves first. Piece classes: every piece is either a Back-rank piece (King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight, Vaulter, Warden, Frostcaller, Longbow, Wraith, Bombard, Lancer) or a Second-rank piece (Pawn, Squire, Shieldbearer); each may only be placed in its own rank. The Paladin is promotion-only (a knighted Squire). Win condition: capture ALL of the enemy's Kings (usually just one). Because abilities (vaults, shots, blasts, freezes) make true checkmate ambiguous, moving into or staying in check is legal; a pulsing red glow warns you when any of your Kings is attacked. Draws: 200 plies, threefold repetition, or no legal moves. Promotion: any piece that started on the second rank (including a Paladin that began as a Squire) that moves onto the far rank promotes to your choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight or King (pick from the pop-up; ✕ cancels). A Warden swap never promotes. Only the original King may castle; Bombards still cannot destroy Kings. Frozen pieces (blue glow ❄) cannot act on their owner's next turn; if every movable piece is frozen the side passes. Board markers: dot = move, red ring = capture, dashed blue = special action (shot, swap, vault-capture, detonate — click the Bombard itself to detonate). AI Elo labels are approximate strength tiers for this variant, not calibrated FIDE/online ratings.
