ℹ️ About & credits

Gambit Forge, an original army-building chess variant. Code, piece art and icon are original to this project.

Coach voices are pre-rendered offline with Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (model and voice packs licensed Apache-2.0), run through the MIT-licensed kokoro-onnx runtime. Per its model card, Kokoro was trained only on permissively licensed, public-domain and synthetic audio. No real person's voice was cloned. The TTS software is not shipped with the game; only the audio it produced is included.

Rules & Piece Glossary

Each side builds an army of 8 back-row and 8 pawn-row pieces with a 40-point budget; exactly one King (free, back row) is mandatory. White moves first. Piece classes: every piece is either a Back-rank piece (all King, Queen, Rook, Bishop and Knight varieties plus the special pieces) or a Second-rank piece (Pawn, Berolina, Sergeant, Shieldbearer, Scout, Runner, Squire, Bulwark); King varieties (King, Warlord, Monarch, Phantom King) all count as Kings, and an army has exactly one; each may only be placed in its own rank. The Paladin is promotion-only (a knighted Squire). Win condition: capture the enemy King. Each side has exactly one King-type piece. Because abilities (vaults, shots, blasts, freezes) make true checkmate ambiguous, moving into or staying in check is legal; a pulsing red glow warns you when your King is attacked. Draws: 200 plies, threefold repetition, or no legal moves. Promotion: any piece that started on the second rank (including a Paladin that began as a Squire) that moves onto the far rank promotes to your choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight, or any of their varieties (never a King) (Empress, Princess, Amazon, Tower, Ram, Warden, Cardinal, Elephant, Priest, Camel, Zebra, Nightrider). Pick from the pop-up; ✕ cancels. A Warden swap never promotes. Only the original King may castle; Bombards still cannot destroy Kings. Frozen pieces (blue glow ❄) cannot act on their owner's next turn; if every movable piece is frozen the side passes. Board markers: dot = move, red ring = capture, dashed blue = special action (shot, swap, vault-capture, detonate — click the Bombard itself to detonate). Coaches have no fixed rating: pick how strongly a coach plays against you (400–2400 Elo, in steps of 100) on their card or on the setup screen; their hints always use full-strength analysis. AI Elo labels are approximate strength tiers for this variant, not calibrated FIDE/online ratings.
