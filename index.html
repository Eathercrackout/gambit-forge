Rules & Piece Glossary

Each side builds an army of 8 back-row and 8 pawn-row pieces with a 40-point budget; exactly one King (free, back row) is mandatory. White moves first. Win condition: capture the enemy King. Because abilities (vaults, shots, blasts, freezes) make true checkmate ambiguous, moving into or staying in check is legal; a pulsing red glow warns you when your King is attacked. Draws: 200 plies, threefold repetition, or no legal moves. Pawns and Shieldbearers reaching the last rank become Queens. Frozen pieces (blue glow ❄) cannot act on their owner's next turn; if every movable piece is frozen the side passes. Board markers: dot = move, red ring = capture, dashed blue = special action (shot, swap, vault-capture, detonate — click the Bombard itself to detonate). AI Elo labels are approximate strength tiers for this variant, not calibrated FIDE/online ratings.
